# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want QuadRix to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- Be respectful.
- Assume good faith.
- No harassment, trolling, or personal attacks.
- No political or off-topic arguments.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to the maintainers. All reports will be reviewed and investigated.
